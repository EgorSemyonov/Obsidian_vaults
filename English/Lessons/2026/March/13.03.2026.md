And these people would be absolutely right!
In justification of this we can claim: "now zoos are getting more ~~comfortable and~~ similar to the wild ~~nature~~ and animals receiving more comfortable conditions. For instance: ...". Actually animals get lots of ==~~receive~~== (care) and ==~~meal~~== food in ~~the~~ zoos and these facts guarantee longer lifespan for animals than in the wild ~~nature~~.
